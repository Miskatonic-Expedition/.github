# Code of Conduct

Miskatonic Expedition is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, and any other behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the organization maintainers. Reports will be
reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove
comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.
